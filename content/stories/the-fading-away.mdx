Someone may be on their deathbed and literally taking their last breaths and they would slowly be fading away as they lose their sight smell hearing and such until eventually they are gone, each part of them died one by one. Their sight died and would not be back, their smell died and would not be back, their taste died and would not be back their hearing died and would not be back. Each died on its own add the person faded away more and not with each part of them that died.

The memory of that person fades away in a similar manner. First their smell is forgotten, then their touch is forgotten, then their voice is forgotten, and finally their face fades out of the memory of those they knew. For some who fade, they fade completely, never known enough by anyone to be remembered. But even for those who are remembered, they are reduced to a shadow. Dull and unclear yet still seen. They shined as the sun in life and cast their own shadow in death. And they too, eventually, fade away.

## Holy judgement on the vampire

The light draws near
The sun shall soon make its appearance
And with it your soul shall sink back into the shadows
Run, flee, disengage from the living who walk the earth
You are not of them
Though that, they do not know
That you are death incarnate does not pass over their shallow minds
The depths of your corruption is not known to them
They mock and jeer and you, knowing the frailty of their lives wait for them to retire to their dark and dismal domicile
For their ignorance they shall pay with the blood of their lives
But you have paid a far greater price for their blood already
For you in your desolation, in the pit of your profundity
Determined your very soul as not but a bargaining chip for your own damnation
And what is this treasure so precious that you would trade your soul into perdition for it?
Not but vanity
Wonder do I, if you even remember this vanity that was worth more than your soul. Or has it faded from your mind, how you've faded from existence amongst the known?
Does the richness of your vanity still pleasure your tongue or has the blood of your fiery, thirsting desire become nothing more than a reminder of death in your mouth.
"It is not what goes into a man that defiles him, but what comes out of a man, that defiles him".
And you oh great defiler, what is the measure of your power
if even you have been defiled by another greater than you
Damnation, willingly you have sought out your own damnation in exchange for not more than to be a worm amongst maggots
Let not your lips cry out for mercy
You neither want it nor know a need for it
Your destruction is welcomed and accepted
Crawl back to the shadows fiend
Pleasure yourself in the vanity of your wicked fascination
Your soul is already abandoned and so too has your very life become a testament to your depravity

## The vampire's response

Despair me not thou holy swine
Go, lie upon your bed and eat the ambrosial slop of thy masters hand.
He beckons you "stay away from the filth of the shadow and night" and wise would you be to obey him promptly.
For you are but an insect and I, the boot of your demise
You cower and make known your pitiful aptitude
But here stand I as the desolation of righteousness
"Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me"
Do not doubt the strength of my hand. You need not trouble your heart in wondering if your very life shall be forfeit, I tell you plainly, it shall.
Believe in me, you worm of the apple, for I am as the tempter of the first fruits of your Father.
Darkness calls me by my name and I by its. For darkness knows me as ruler and so I am.
Who has defiled me? I am as I desire. In my profundity did I indeed make an end to the soul which once plagued my existence.
I need not the bleeding proffering of forgiveness and mercy, but it is I who takes grace and extirpates it to nothing.
The blood of humanity is on my hands and I shall savor every morsel of it.
Watch in dismay as I take the life which you have deemed so precious and drain it for my wicked fascination.
There is no vanity that I have found, but all is venerated in the clutches of my depravity.
Look upon the depths, see the life which the leviathan has consumed. See, and know that the leviathan is not but my watching dog, it is I who commands him and it is I at whose feet he drops the corpses of the children of your Father.
Grovel and cower, flee and forget, you would do good to obey your master, oh you, mongrel of the alter. Stay away from my filth, my wicked attraction.
Do not venerate me in the house of your Lord, lest you fall and become as a dog without its master.
Then, I shall neither pick you up, nor shall I end your putrid suffering. But you shall be left as a lost sheep which the Shepard did not find. Then shall your life escape you and you be made as carrion for the vultures.

## The priest

My God, my God, why hast Thou forsaken me?
Have I not spoken Your name amongst many, leading them to the Fountain of Living Water?
Have I not dispelled demons from amongst the hopeless and forgotten?
Have I not gloried You in all my doings?
Why then have I been brought to the place of my own ruin.
One as satan stands before me, and I, I am not but dust in the winds of his breath.
Dearest Adonai, Maker and Master, deliver me, my soul clings to Your garments.
See me in my hour of need and remember the glory I have given You in times past. When the world laid up the people of Your church, it was I who brought them deliverance in Your Name.
The depths of the bottomless pit are known in looking upon the creature before me, I am a stone cast into the pit.
Let not the clutches of the Great Beast pull me asunder
Oh dearest Ancient of Days, by Your hand You have blessed Your faithful servant, and by my hand I have blessed the branches of Your vine.
Forget me not in the valley, I stand in the shadow of an Evil unparalleled.
I know not by what sin I have offended the Father of my soul, but teach me Your ways oh Lord that I may walk in Your truth, and surly goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life.
You oh Good Shepherd shall leave the ninety nine for the one.
I pray You, oh Shepherd of my soul, will find Your lost sheep and bring him back into Your light and glory.
"My heart is in anguish within me;
the terrors of death have fallen upon me.
Fear and trembling come upon me,
and horror overwhelms me."
I am overcome by the terror of the night, but You, Adonai, You shall deliver me.
You have deemed Me worthy of sitting at Your right hand, Your life has been given to Me as a gift and I have accepted. You have graced Me with the power of Your presence and I shall not fall prey to this Worker of iniquity.
Surely You Lord, will uphold Me with Your righteous right hand.
Strengthen My heart and steady My feet. I shall not fear for You are with Me.
"Thy word is a lamp unto My feet, and a light unto My path."
You oh God, have known My heart, You alone have seen the reaches of My hands for your glory. I shall not be brought down by the shadow of death.
Strengthen the rod of your servant, O lord, and through Me shall the Angel who was cast down be vanquished. By My hand, you shall cast Him into the lake of fire.

## The vampire's response to the priest

Is this your hope?
That you should call upon a master whom you have never seen?
Believe you me, I have seen.
Pitiful are your cries and disquieted is the conviction of your soul.
Your God shall not incline His ear to your distress.
You have been forgotten, abandoned by the One for whom you bled.
Despair oh you wicked of heart, for you are not more to your Father than an orphan to the mother whom they never knew.
And perhaps they escape you, the words of your so called "Lord", but here I shall make known your desolation.
"Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in thy name? and in thy name have cast out devils? and in thy name done many wonderful works? And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity."
Hear the words of your God and remember the words of your own disingenuous heart.
"Have I not spoken your name amongst many, leading them to the Fountain of living water?
Have I not dispelled demons from amongst the hopeless and forgotten?
Have I not gloried you in all my doings?"
Art thou afraid?
Do you see now the desolation of your soul?
You have been rejected by a God whom you never knew. Abandoned by he whom you falsely worshipped. And for the putrescence of your iniquity have you been brought before me.
"My God my God, why hast thou forsaken me" in your quivering terror have you mimicked the carpenter. And yet your house was built on sand.
Your terror is rightly founded though. For I have come that you may have death.
Your life has been forfeit and the surety of your destruction made known to you.
For what purpose didst ye serve thy master, how sickly is the reward of the holy.
For your remembrance of the Father, for the works of your hands, for the dedication of your prayers, you have been paid with ruination.
Tell me, was your life made abundant or has your diligence led you to naught but suffering?
If not suffering before, then be sure that your suffering shall be brought to you by my hand.
I shall make you as the shadow you so feared. You will know the fullness of your destruction. Look upon the form that stands before you, look and fall upon thy face. Eat the dirt and raise it upon thy trembling hands as an offering to the God whom you seek. I tell you now, the god of this world has set his eyes on you. You have become a sacrifice, a lamb slain upon the alter of depravity.
Your soul is cast down. Your life, seized by the clutches of death. How now shall you cry out to your God, oh you ember of the flame. Your name has been forgotten.

## The judge to the priest

"Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you are like whitewashed tombs, which outwardly appear beautiful, but within are full of dead people's bones and all uncleanness."
How like the Pharisees you are, oh you viper among lambs.
The hour of your reckoning has come.
Behold the iniquity of your meritless life.
How you have callously cast aside the grace of the Father.
How secretly you have hidden the vain desires of your heart
How rotten was the root of your works
See your iniquity and tear thy garments because of your shame
For you are but dust
Tell me, ye of false glory, where are the treasures you vainly piled now?
Will they save you?
Will the grandeur of your actions deliver your soul from retribution?
You have tasted much, yet the bread of life did not appease your tongue.
With many sacred words did you speak life amongst the perishing.
From your wretched mouth came the sanctity of living speech.
Yet you have put on iniquity as a robe.
Upon the poor was the burden of your plenty. With their offerings you did satisfy your lusts
Your greed brought the forgotten to their knees
You dressed yourself with a cloak of fine works and righteous deeds
Yet The Most High counted them to you as nothing
You wore your works as a precious crown
"Have I not spoken Your name amongst many, leading them to the Fountain of Living Water?"
Indeed you did, and how full was the measure of your pride when the lost came calling you "rabbi", "teacher", "master".
How wicked was your heart in leading the sheep to the well from which you dared not drink.
Sift through the dust of your works.
Tell me, is there even one righteous deed for you to claim
Or do you now know the reaches of your demise
Your shame is upon you and bought with your doings
With your own tongue you did speak your soul into perdition
With your own hands you have dug your grave
Indeed the ground cries out for your blood
Thou wicked man, fall upon thy face. Eat the dirt and raise it upon thy trembling hands as an offering to the God who knows you. Blessed are you O wretched sheep, for the Good Shepherd shall not let even one of his own be lost
You in the iniquity of your works, did yet call upon the Son of Man
But for his grace, you would be bound to a most woeful fate
But the grace of the Most High be upon you
For the God of all mercy calls you beloved
Your deeds were filth and your heart was one of rot
Yet Adonai did watch you in your pursuit of Him
And by his love He counted that one thing to you as righteousness
Your name has been eternally known in His book of life
You shall not be forgotten

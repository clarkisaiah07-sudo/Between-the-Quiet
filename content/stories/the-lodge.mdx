## Wednesday September 22nd.

From the front steps of the lodge, I could see the town far below. The forest surrounding it growing darker as evening was swallowed up by night. The lodge stood behind me. Although I'd stood in that spot many times before, tonight felt different. The wooden walls seemed to climb far higher than they had before. The lights seemed to not have as much effect against the darkness. Even the grass seemed deeper, as if, were I to step off the stairs and back into the grass it would pull me down into itself.

"Are you ready brother" my brother said to me.

"I am" I replied.

They wrapped the red silk around my head, covering my eyes. My hands they bound in front of me with the same silk fabric.

I took a deep breath, the smell of dew and fire smoke in the evening filling my nose.

My brothers led me to the front doors and knocked quickly three times with one pause and followed by one more knock. The heavy doors opened and with one brother on each side of me, I was led through the open space of the lodge.

I'd been through it so many times that even with my eyes covered, I could perfectly picture the space.

Dark wooden walls with antlers and giant portraits lining them. The large glass and antler chandelier hung above the center of the floor. Stairs wrapped around both sides of the room, leading up to the second story. However that was not where we were heading. We went straight through the room and stopped in front of the large, heavy red double door that stood in the center of the wall opposite of the front doors.

My brothers now knocked on these doors in synch with each other. The knock was the opposite of the first.

One knock then a pause, followed by three quick knocks.

The doors opened silently, but its heaviness seemed to change the air as it opened.

My brothers traded sides of me and with their outermost hands held my shoulders and walked me through the doors.

I was led down stairs I'd never before walked, and each step down felt heavier than the last, like my body weighed more the lower down I went.

By the time we'd reached the last stair, my body felt ready to collapse with the weight of itself.

And upon stepping off the last step and onto the floor below, I was met with a near weightlessness. Like my whole body took a breath after being submerged in deep water.

I could smell a fire burning somewhere, and the faint smell of vanilla on the air. The wood cracking in the fireplace was the only noise in the room. The air was soft and thick in my ears, and sound moved cautiously through it.

"Who stands here?" Said a voice in front of me.

"One who has known only darkness, and now seeks light" said my brothers in unison.

A bag was then placed over my entire head.

"Who stands here?" The voice said again.

"One who does not know the light, but has grown wearisome of darkness".

Gentle, yet firm, hands led me to my knees.

"Who kneels here?"

"One who is not, but desires to be". My brothers said.

A hooded robe was then draped over me and tied.

"Who were you"

"I was one of the lost. Seeking light in dark place" I said.

"Where were you found"

"In the pit"

"Where shall you go"

"Into the light"

"What is your name?"

"I have no name"

"How are you found"

"I am found wanting"

"What do you desire"

"A name"

"Will you pay the price a name will cost you?"

"I will"

"Will you love the price of a name"

"I will"

"What will a name cost you?"

"A name will cost me all"

"And you will pay all"

"I will"

"when all is paid, you shall receive your name"

Cool sharp metal then touched my back and the robe was cut off me from behind.

Still blindfolded I was led backwards towards the stairs and backwards up them. No sensation of heaviness occurred this time. When we reached the top stair, my brothers again traded sides and led me through the doors.

Once we were back in the common area of the lodge, my hands were untied. I was instructed to wait until the I heard a door close again before I was to remove my blindfold. Then I was to fold up my binding and place it on the table in front of the fireplace.

After hearing the door close, I did as I was instructed.

And then looking around the room, I saw that I was alone.

I'm up in my room, drinking tea now as I record this. The mint is a little too strong, tomorrow I'll add honey.

## Thursday September 23rd

It was cloudy this morning. The smell of fall leaves filled the forest as I drove in the truck, making my rounds. The day was typical of a Thursday morning, Margret had coffee made back at the station, and there weren't any particularly pressing reports or duty's to attend to. So mostly I spent the shift just out maintaining the grounds. Around lunch time it started to drizzle, and I came up on a large tree that had fallen onto the dirt road. I got out and grabbed the chainsaw and cut the tree into movable chunks. And as I was cutting, I noticed that while I could feel the rumble of the chainsaw in my hands, and I could feel the wood chips hitting them, I could not feel the rain on them. I watched as rain fell on the backs of my hands, but there was no sensation of impact or even wetness. As I record this, I still don't know what to make of that. But the rest of the work day went on as normal. I didn't get out of the truck again after moving the tree, and by the time I got back to the station, the rain had stopped, though it's still cloudy even now.

I'm cleaned up now and about to head to dinner with my brothers.

## Thursday September 23rd (updated)

Dinner was strange. No one spoke. We sat at the large oval wooden table in the lodge, and the girls brought out our meal. No one said thank you, no one moved to eat. No one moved at all. Everyone sat still until the clock struck 7, then everyone moved in unison and began eating. I tried to keep up, and just follow their lead. And when it was over one by one each brother stood up, walked around the table to me and set a silver coin down in front of me. Then they walked out, each leaving the room before the next stood to bring me their coin.

After they'd all left I sat there for a bit. One of the girls stood in the doorway that I assume leads to the kitchen. She didn't move to gather any dishes or anything at all until I stood up and left. I'm back in my room now. The tea is still too minty, even with honey. Maybe I'll just drink milk tomorrow night.

## Friday September 24th

*(Note to self. Lock the door tonight)*

I dreamt of a red room. Deep wine red. I think it was supposed to have been a room in the lodge, due to the familiar wood walls and scent of vanilla. The room was empty except a large chair in front of the fire place. I sat in the chair and a thought occurred to me that I should place my hand in the fire. I did and just as with the rain, there was no sensation, no heat or pain. I brought my hand out and saw that the hairs had been singed off and the skin was lightly burned. Upon waking I looked at my hand and saw that indeed the hairs are singed and there are light burns on it.

Also I remember something said to me in the dream, but I don't remember who said it. "They hate the light".

I don't work today, I am meeting the brothers again for what they call my "last rites".
